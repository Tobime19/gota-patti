Research Inquiries & Academic Correspondence

This digital archive is developed as a Bachelor of Design (Communication Design) college research assignment. We welcome inquiries from textile design scholars, museum curators, and craft conservationists.

Project Scope & Statement

Student Design Research Project

This project represents independent academic research into the tangible and intangible heritage of Rajasthani Gota Patti. It does not operate as a commercial enterprise or claim official state certification. All analyses adhere to ethical design research methodologies and academic fair-use guidelines.

Curriculum: Bachelor of Design (B.Des)
Discipline: Communication Design
Focus: Indian Traditional Craft Documentation
Documented Craft Geographies

Jaipur & Shekhawati Craft Clusters

Cluster 01: Johari Bazaar & Kishanpole 26.9205° N, 75.8236° E • Jaipur Urban District

Historic market center for gold wire drawing (badla), tinsel ribbons, and wholesale raw materials.

Cluster 02: Nayla Village Craft Hub 26.8521° N, 75.9814° E • Jaipur Rural Sub-District

Key center of home-based women artisans specializing in micro-tacking and contemporary bridal sarees.

Cluster 03: Khandela & Sikar 27.6094° N, 75.4981° E • Shekhawati Region

Lineage workshops recognized for intricate Seekhi Gota and historic geometric border traditions.
