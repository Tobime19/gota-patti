CURATORIAL MONOGRAPH // FOLIO CHAPTER IV

The Geometry of Light: How the Noor Jahan Was Created

In the royal courts of 17th-century Amber Fort, royal robes were not simply garments; they were luminous astronomical devices. Under candlelight, the angled facets of beaten gold ribbons refracted radiance at exactly 45-degree inclinations, creating a shimmering aura around the wearer.

The Noor Jahan saree re-establishes this forgotten courtly science. Every individual lotus rosette (*Kamal*) requires 48 distinct hand-folded metallic ribbon petals. Unlike modern machine lace, each petal is cut on the true bias from hand-hammered gold ribbons (*Gota Tukdi*) and pinched with warmed iron pincers to hold its structural bloom.

brush

The 4-Step Imperial Technique

01 / Chhapayi

Calcium carbonate stenciling applied through hand-perforated tracing parchment.

02 / Tukdi Gadhana

Cold-pressing the silver ribbon between brass shears into petal rhomboids.

03 / Tankaayi

Invisible blind-stitch anchoring using matched raw silk filament thread.

04 / Moti Tankna

Natural Basra-style seed pearl couching along the floral perimeter.
