ATELIER NAYLA

Radhika Singhal

Specializing in Chandi Ki Gota (Pure Beaten Silver Ribbons) & Khadi Tissue Silk Appliqué.

Craft Approach & Metallurgy verified_user

Rejecting synthetic aluminum and plastic-backed lurex foils, the atelier commissions bespoke ribbons drawn from 99.2% pure silver ingots hammered into gossamer strips (badla), woven on traditional hand-cranked wooden pit looms in the Johari Bazaar guild.

Alloy Ratio 92.5% Ag / 7.5% Cu
Artisan Guilds Kukas & Sanganer (42 karigars)
