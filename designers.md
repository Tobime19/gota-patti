## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a rarefied synthesis of museum-grade craft curation and modern Indian luxury editorial design. It preserves and showcases the timeless art of *Gota Patti*—an appliqué technique born in Rajasthan involving hammered gold and silver ribbons woven into motifs of flora, fauna, and cosmic geometry.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Quiet Heritage Luxury**: Restrained, respectful, and authoritative. It replaces heavy ornamental cliché with intentional white space, disciplined typography, and archival purity.
- **Editorial Archival Rigor**: Drawing visual cues from architectural monographs, *Architectural Digest India*, and contemporary haute couture archives (Raw Mango, Sabyasachi Heritage Foundation). 
- **Tactile Materiality**: The interface evokes hand-pressed paper, raw tussar silk, and hand-beaten metallic leaf via nuanced tonal shifts and hair-thin rule lines.

### Emotional Response
Users must feel reverence, stillness, and an intimate connection to the human hand behind the craft. Every viewport should read like a page from an artisanal luxury folio.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in organic dyes, aged metallic leaf, and handloom parchment.

- **Primary (`#5A1720` - Heritage Maroon)**: Derived from aged alizarin dye. Reserved for editorial hero highlights, key CTA buttons, archive taxonomy badges, and primary hover states.
- **Secondary (`#B89B5E` - Antique Brushed Gold)**: A matte, patinated gold leaf. Applied strictly to fine architectural rule lines, foil-stamped sub-headers, motif outline accents, and micro-delimiters. Never used as heavy fills or loud gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#241A16` - Deep Charcoal Umber)**: The deep tone of smoked woodblocks and archival ink. Used for dark contrast feature spreads, quote cards, evening editorial viewports, and primary display typography.
- **Neutral Surface Tier**:
  - `Base Canvas`: Warm Ivory (`#F7F1E8`) evokes handmade Khadi paper.
  - `Layered Container`: Muted Raw Silk Beige (`#EADECF`) and Sand Parchment (`#D8C8B5`) for subtle section transitions and card elevations without shadow.
  - `Text Neutral`: Deep Iron Umber (`#2A2421`) for body text, ensuring warm high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure black.

## Typography

The typography uses **Inter** (`https://rsms.me/inter/`) throughout the entire website to combine modern editorial clarity with archival legibility:

- **Inter SemiBold / Bold (`600` / `700`)**: Used for display titles, section headings, pull-quotes, and chapter indices with refined negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em`).
- **Inter Regular (`400`)**: Provides high-contrast legibility across technical weave specifications, motif provenance coordinates, curator narratives, and body reading copy.
- **Inter Medium (`500`)**: Set in all-caps `label-md` and `label-sm` with generous letter tracking (`0.12em` to `0.15em`) to evoke museum collection tags, serial identification codes, and fine gallery labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the deliberate cadence of open-spread art monographs. Content breathes through disciplined wide gutters, asymmetric column groupings, and deliberate negative space.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px to 1440px max-width content container with `margin: 4rem` and `gutter: 2.5rem`.
  - Editorial hero features utilize asymmetrical splits (e.g., 7 columns for macro textile imagery, 5 columns for archival provenance and artisan lineage).
  - Catalogue grids follow 3-column or 4-column balanced modules punctuated by full-span artisan narrative quotes.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Reflows to symmetrical 4-column pairing; margins scale to `2.5rem`.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, single-column narrative stacks, and horizontal scroll carousels for motif galleries with peek margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section transitions use expansive spacing (`space-xl` scaled to `5rem`–`7.5rem` for major chapters) delimited by ultra-fine rule lines (`0.5px` to `1px` Antique Gold or Sand Parchment) rather than heavy bounding boxes.

## Elevation & Depth

To protect the authentic, tactile quality of hand-crafted textiles, traditional volumetric drop shadows and blur-heavy floating effects are discarded. Depth is created purely through tone, paper-like layering, and fine line craft.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Tonal Stepping**: 
  - Ground layer: Warm Ivory (`#F7F1E8`).
  - Elevated containment (Cards, Drawer panels, Tooltips): Muted Sand Beige (`#EADECF`) or Deep Charcoal Umber (`#241A16`).
  - Active overlays: Translucent Warm Parchment (`rgba(247, 241, 232, 0.94)`) combined with a crisp `1px` Antique Brushed Gold perimeter border.
- **Hairline Framing**: Rather than elevation shadows, components use `0.5px` or `1px` borders in `#B89B5E` (Antique Gold at 30%–60% opacity) or `#D8C8B5` (Sand Beige). This replicates gold wire thread (*kalabattu*) and bookbinder borders.
- **Floating Controls**: Floating navigation elements or bottom sheets employ a whisper ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(36, 26, 22, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The architectural structure demands an uncompromising **0 (Sharp)** foundational geometry. 

- **Sharp Rectilinear Form (`0px` border radius)**: Cards, image frames, technical data tables, and structural layout panels maintain crisp, precision-cut corners. This references museum archival mounts, fine art framing, and uncut fabric bolts.
- **Controlled Pill Exceptions**: Reserved strictly for micro-tag labels, timeline node indicators, interactive motif selection pills, and secondary button chips (`rounded-full`) to deliver tactile contrast against the rigid framework.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Deep Heritage Maroon (`#5A1720`) background, Warm Ivory (`#F7F1E8`) text, sharp corners (`0px`), uppercase `label-md` with `0.12em` tracking. Padding: `1rem 2.25rem`. Hover introduces an Antique Gold outline with a gentle interior shift to `#451118`.
- **Secondary / Archival Action**: Transparent background with a `1px` solid Antique Brushed Gold (`#B89B5E`) hairline border, Deep Charcoal Umber text, sharp edges. Hover fills surface with `#EADECF`.
- **Ghost Editorial Link**: Bodoni Moda or high-tracking sans text accompanied by an elongated thin rule arrow (`→`), underlined with a subtle `1px` antique gold hairline that expands from center on hover.

### Product & Archive Cards
- **Artifact Tile**: Sharp image container set against `#EADECF` or `#F7F1E8`. Images are framed with a hairline `0.5px` border. On hover, the image engages a restrained zoom (1.03x scale over 600ms ease-out) with an Antique Gold badge revealing regional provenance (e.g., *“Jaipur, circa 1920”*).
- **Price & Metadata Display**: Product titles set in `headline-sm`, complemented by price in INR (e.g., `₹ 84,000`) formatted in `label-lg` with subtle gold accentuation.

### Chips & Taxonomy Filters
- Curated pill tabs (`padding: 0.5rem 1.25rem`) finished with an ultra-thin border (`#D8C8B5`). 
- Active state transitions to Deep Charcoal Umber (`#241A16`) with Ivory typography and an Antique Gold indicator dot.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields**: Minimalist bottom-line or framed field in Warm Ivory, accented with an Antique Gold bottom line (`1px`). When focused, the line deepens to Heritage Maroon (`#5A1720`) with floating `label-sm` labels.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square (`0px`) checkboxes with a hairline border. Active check is an artisanal antique gold dot or minimal geometric fill.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Motif Inspector**: An interactive interactive zoom tile showcasing intricate *Gota Patti* stitches (e.g., *Phool*, *Champa*, *Keri/Paisley*, *Bakhandi*). Displays x-ray style thread density, metal alloy ratio (Gold/Silver/Gilded Copper), and master artisan notes.
- **Artisan Testimonial Folio**: Deep Charcoal Umber (`#241A16`) backdrop, centered display quotation in `Bodoni Moda` italic, with golden artisan hallmark signature stamp and geographic origin coordinates.
- **Process Timeline**: A vertical or horizontal continuous hairline of Antique Gold punctuated by solid square nodes documenting the 6 stages of creation: *Tracing (Chhapayi)*, *Folding (Gota Tukdi)*, *Tacking (Tankaayi)*, and *Finishing*.
